## Replay (E10)
- ReplayBufferFile, ReplayFrameExtractor and ReplayBuffer are kept Kotlin (com.moblin.android.various, declared `internal`): call them exactly as declared; `ReplayBufferFile(url: video.url(), …)` -> `ReplayBufferFile(url = video.url().path, duration = …, remove = …)`, and `ReplayBufferFile.url` is a String path, never a URL or File. A declaration that takes a ReplayBufferFile is `internal` too (`class ReplayEffect internal constructor(...)`, `class ReplayEffectReplayReader internal constructor(...)`).
- `CIFilter.dissolveTransition()` -> `CIFilter.dissolveTransition()` (a CITransitionFilter, com.moblin.android.platform.coreimage) with `inputImage`, `targetImage` and `time` (Float); its `outputImage` is nullable. `CMVideoDimensions` is android.util.Size (Int width and height); `CMTime(seconds: s)` -> `(s * 1_000_000.0).toLong()` microseconds; `asset.duration()` is the AVAsset member (Double seconds), never the MediaMetadataRetriever extension in Utils.kt.
