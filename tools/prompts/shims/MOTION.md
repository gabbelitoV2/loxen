## Device motion and attitude (MOTION, package com.moblin.android.platform.coremotion)
- `CMMotionManager()`, `deviceMotionUpdateInterval`, `stopDeviceMotionUpdates()` keep their shape; `startDeviceMotionUpdates(to: OperationQueue.main) { data, _ in }` -> `startDeviceMotionUpdates(to = OperationQueue.main) { data, _ -> }`. `OperationQueue.main` runs the handler on the main thread; the handler never runs after `stopDeviceMotionUpdates()`, which unregisters the sensors.
- `data.attitude` is a `CMAttitude` with `roll`, `pitch` and `yaw` (radians, iOS axes and Euler order, yaw from an arbitrary start) and `quaternion` (`CMQuaternion` x, y, z, w). A stored `CMAttitude?` keeps its type, never `Any?`. There is no `rotationMatrix` or `multiply(byInverseOf:)`.
- There are no accelerometer updates (`startAccelerometerUpdates`, `CMAccelerometerData`): Model's `motionManager` stays the `SensorManager?` that `GForceManager(sensorManager:)` takes, and Model's device motion (`startMotionDetection`, `stopMotionDetection`) uses `private val manualFocusMotionManager = com.moblin.android.platform.coremotion.CMMotionManager()`, declared on the line after `motionManager`.
